Objective

Transform 30 young women into entrepreneurs — converting 2500 years of textile tradition into a business model.

📍

Location

Tashkent, Uzbekistan — capital and cultural center. Silk Road cultural heritage city.

⏱️

Duration

6 months — October 2026 to March 2027. 8 activities, 12 training modules.

Initial situation

"Kelaghayi sells for five-ten manats, its real value is fifty-hundred manats. The lack of business skills means heritage doesn't convert to income."

Phases

4 phases, 6 months

1
01.10.2026 – 15.11.2026

Participant selection and survey

30 women selected from over 200 applications (15 Az and 15 Uz). Economic survey among over 200 neighborhood women.

2
18.11.2026 – 20.11.2026

3-day intensive training — Tashkent

12 modules: cultural branding, tourist sales, Instagram/Etsy, digital marketing, craft exchange.

3
21.11.2026

Market day — Tashkent

Over 50 buyers and distributors. Sales protocols and long-term contracts.

4
01.12.2026 – 31.03.2027

Booklet, Virtual mentoring and Network

490 copies of booklet to schools. 3-month online mentoring. Women's Cultural Heritage Network on Telegram.

📚

12 Training Modules

Branding, tourist sales, Instagram, Etsy, pricing, digital marketing, business model, exchange, buyer relations.

🤝

Joint Exchange

Kelaghayi and ikat combination — unique brand. Two countries' craft traditions together for the first time.

📱

Online Sales

Etsy, Instagram Shop, WhatsApp Business. Goal: 15 participants start selling within 6 months.

🏆

Certificate and Network

30 certified participants. Telegram network, 6 months continuous. Mentor-mentee, weekly consultations.

Video filming and innovation

Documenting cultural heritage through film

The project's main objective — documenting Silk Road heritage through professional video for future generations.

🎬

Filming location — Samarkand

Samarkand Registan — UNESCO World Heritage Site №603. Craft processes are documented.

🎥

What we film

Kelaghayi printing, suzani embroidery, carpet weaving, ikat making. Artisan stories and neighborhood life.

🤖

Use of AI

AI in editing: subtitles (4 languages), audio cleaning, color correction. Creative decisions by humans.

🏛️

Preserving heritage

Video documentation is the main objective — 2500 years of textile traditions are passed to the future not only through live demonstration but also through film. Every artisan's story, every technique's details, every pattern's meaning is recorded.
