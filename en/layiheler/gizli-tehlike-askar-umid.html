About the project

Knowing the danger —
is what saves lives

Mines and unexploded ordnance left in the liberated territories still put the daily lives of young people, their routes to school and their workplaces at risk. This project reduces that danger while equipping young people with the skills to build a future.

Goal

To raise mine-safety awareness among young people in liberated Karabakh and prepare them for work with vocational, entrepreneurship and digital skills — then connect them to employment through a job fair and a mentoring programme.

📍

Where

Agdam, Fuzuli, Khojavend and Shusha districts — delivered jointly with the local executive authorities and municipalities of all four districts.

⏱️

Duration

6 months — 1 January to 30 June 2027. 11 activities, five months of intensive training and a mentoring programme.

The problem

"The land has been liberated — but the danger is still there. There are no youth, no participants, no skills. If young people do not learn to recognise the danger and do not acquire skills, building a new life is impossible."

Activities

11 activities, 6 months

1
01.01.2027 – 31.01.2027

Preparatory phase and participant selection

Mobilising the project team and specialists, signing agreements with partners, selecting 184 participants, running the baseline survey and needs assessment, preparing training materials.

2
01.02.2027 – 28.02.2027

Mine and unexploded ordnance safety awareness

Sessions led by a certified specialist on safe behaviour rules, identifying risk zones and emergency response; production and distribution of awareness booklets.

3
01.02.2027 – 30.04.2027

Intensive vocational and entrepreneurship training — 60 youth

8 training days: vocational skills, business plan writing, financial literacy, marketing. Every participant works on an individual business idea; certificates are awarded at the end.

4
01.02.2027 – 30.04.2027

Digital literacy training

Practical workshops on digital tools, online services, e-commerce and social media marketing — widening young people's access to online work and sales.

🎓

Wide-format seminars — 124 youth

Short seminars on employment, entrepreneurship and safety for young people outside the intensive group. 1 February – 30 April 2027.

🧠

Psychosocial support sessions

Group and individual sessions with a professional psychologist on post-conflict stress, psychological resilience and safety anxiety. Target: positive change in at least 70% of participants. 1 February – 31 May 2027.

7
01.04.2027 – 31.05.2027

Job fair and meetings with employers

One job fair with local employers and entrepreneurs; CV preparation and interview skills; internships and vacancies. Target: at least 35 young people employed or self-employed within 3–6 months.

8
01.03.2027 – 30.06.2027

Young leaders programme and community initiatives

Mentoring support for 10–12 young leaders; mentoring and technical support for 3 youth initiatives; at least 8 community events organised by the leaders.

🎬

Documentary film — 60 minutes

Shooting and editing a documentary about the new life of young people, with Azerbaijani, English and Italian subtitles. The film carries the real stories of project participants. 1 February – 31 May 2027.

📺

Premiere and screenings

Premiere in Azerbaijan and at least one international screening (Italy, via a partner platform); online release and media coverage. Target: ~5000 viewers. 1 June – 30 June 2027.

📊

Monitoring and reporting

Continuous monitoring: participant registry, pre/post surveys, indicator tracking; interim and final evaluation, and preparation of donor financial and programme reports. January – June 2027.

Ownership and responsibility

The project is delivered by the Support for Solidarity People Public Union (GTDİB) and submitted to the small grant competition of the Ministry of Education and the Agency for State Support of NGOs. All activities follow the principles of protecting participants' personal data and voluntary participation.
