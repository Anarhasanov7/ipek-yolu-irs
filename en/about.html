About Us

"Support for Solidarity People" Public Union — a youth NGO active since 2003.

About the organization

Supporting youth, caring for heritage

"Support for Solidarity People" Public Union was registered on May 30, 2003, in Baku. The organization's main goal is to support the socio-economic development, education, and cultural heritage preservation of youth.

We believe that youth are the future of society, and providing them with the necessary skills and opportunities is the foundation of sustainable development. The organization participates in both local and international projects, including the European Union's Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps programmes.

Mission & Values

Our mission and principles

Supporting youth, preserving cultural heritage, and developing international cooperation.

🎯

Mission

To support the socio-economic development, education, and vocational training of youth; to preserve the cultural heritage of Azerbaijan and the region for future generations; and to broaden young people's horizons through international cooperation.

💡

Vision

A society where every young person has equal opportunities to realize their potential.

⚖️

Equality

Equal opportunities for everyone, regardless of gender, nationality, or social background.

🌱

Sustainability

Long-term impact and sustainable development. Not quick fixes, but lasting change.

🤝

Solidarity

Serving the community and supporting youth. Mutual responsibility and support.

📚

Education & Development

Lifelong learning principle. Equipping youth with new skills, organizing vocational and entrepreneurship training. Digital skills, business modeling, and marketing education.

Areas of Activity

What we do

Education, cultural heritage, international cooperation, and youth development.

🎓

Youth Education & Training

Vocational training, entrepreneurship skills, digital literacy, and business model training. Supporting youth integration into the labor market.

🏛️

Cultural Heritage Preservation

Documenting and preserving Azerbaijani carpet weaving, kelaghayi, suzani, and other traditional crafts.

🌍

International Projects

Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps programmes. Youth exchanges, volunteering, and partnership projects.

🤝

Social Support

Helping youth integrate into social life. Solidarity and volunteer activities.
