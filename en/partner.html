Why partner with GTDIB?

We build consortia and run joint projects

GTDIB is an Azerbaijani NGO active since 2003 in youth support, education and cultural heritage. We participate in the European Union's Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps (ESC) programmes, and we build consortia with international partners to prepare and deliver joint projects.

EU

Erasmus+ projects

Youth exchanges (KA1), strategic partnerships (KA2), capacity-building and other Erasmus+ calls — building consortia and applying together.

🤝

European Solidarity Corps (ESC)

Volunteering projects for young people — short- and long-term ESC volunteering, volunteer teams and solidarity projects. We are looking for partner organizations.

🏛️

Cultural heritage & education

Joint projects on cultural heritage preservation, non-formal education, vocational training and social inclusion of youth. We bring Azerbaijan and South Caucasus experience together with European partners.

📋

Project design & management

Project concept development, budgeting, reporting and dissemination — an experienced team across all stages of the consortium.

Who do we partner with?

NGOs and youth organizations — working on youth support, volunteering and social inclusion;
Education institutions — universities, vocational schools and non-formal education providers;
Culture & heritage organizations — museums, archives, cultural heritage custodians;
International partners — consortia with organizations from EU countries, South Caucasus and Eastern Europe.

GTDIB is registered in the Erasmus+/ESC Organisation Registration system (OID: E10439176). Registration in the Republic of Azerbaijan: 1103-Q1-1443 (30/05/2003).
