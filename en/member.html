Membership

Become a Member

Join the "Support for Solidarity People" Public Union as an individual member. Activities in youth support, education and cultural heritage.

Why join GTDIB?

We work together for youth support and cultural heritage

GTDIB is a youth organization active since 2003. Our members participate in social inclusion of young people, vocational training, cultural heritage preservation and international projects. You can contribute to this work too.

🌍

International projects

Opportunity to take part in Erasmus+ youth exchanges, ESC volunteering and other international projects. Represent Azerbaijan and work with European and South Caucasus partners.

📚

Training & capacity building

Trainings, seminars and workshops using non-formal education methods. Gain experience in professional skills, leadership, project writing and youth work.

🏛️

Cultural heritage & community

Participate in cultural heritage promotion, carpet art, traditional crafts and community projects. Help bring Azerbaijani culture to the world.

🤝

Volunteering & network

Join a network of volunteers fighting loneliness and supporting young people. Meet socially responsible people who create change.

What does membership mean?

Individual membership — participate in GTDIB's activities, vote at general assemblies and help shape the organization's direction.
Active participation — take part in projects, events and trainings; contribute in areas that match your interests.
Development opportunities — trainings, international exchanges and certificates (Erasmus+ Youthpass, etc.).
Network — connect with people working in youth work in Azerbaijan and internationally.

Membership is free of charge. Anyone aged 18 and above can join. After your application, our team will contact you to discuss the next steps.

Partner as an organization

Your application will be recorded and our team will contact you. Your information is not shared with third parties. You can also reach us via the contact page.
